UnicornPlayer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

UnicornPlayer (the "App") is a local music player. We (an independent developer) take your privacy seriously and process information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

The App requires no registration or sign-in and integrates no analytics, advertising, or push-notification SDKs. Nearly all data generated while using the App stays on your device.

2. How We Collect and Use Information

2.1 Information kept on your device only (never uploaded)

  • Local music library: When scanning the audio files you grant access to, the App reads tags stored in the files themselves (title, artist, album, duration, embedded cover art) to display and play them.
  • Your usage data: Play history, playlists, favorites, theme and language preferences, playback position, etc., are stored exclusively in the App's local data directory on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2.2 Network features (only when you use the corresponding function)

  • Update check: On startup, the App connects to the update source (api.github.com) to query for a new version. The request carries no personal identity information and uploads none of your local data.
  • Lyrics search: When you actively use the "Search lyrics" function, the App sends the song's title and artist name to the lyrics service lrclib.net to find matching lyrics; downloaded lyrics are saved only to the Download/Unicorn/Lyrics/ folder on your device. If you never use this function, no related network request is made.

2.3 What we do not do

  • We do not collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, OAID, etc.);
  • We do not access precise location, contacts, SMS, or call logs;
  • We do not build user profiles or serve personalized ads;
  • We do not share any of your information with marketing third parties.

You can adjust these grants anytime in device "Settings → Apps → UnicornPlayer → Permissions", or read details in the App under "Settings → Permission list".

4. Local Data Storage and Deletion

All App data (library, playlists, play history, settings) is stored locally on your device; we provide no cloud backup. You may clear the corresponding data within the App at any time, and uninstalling the App removes all data permanently.

5. Third-party SDK List

The App uses several open-source components. For details on which components perform network communication and what data is involved, see the Third-party SDK List.

6. Your Rights

  • You may revoke any of the above permissions in system settings at any time; the related function becomes unavailable while others keep working;
  • You may delete playlists, play history, and other local data within the App at any time;
  • To remove all data completely, simply uninstall the App.

7. Minors

The App collects no personal information from anyone, including minors. If a minor uses the App under a guardian's knowledge, the guardian may manage related permissions according to this policy.

8. Updates to This Policy

If this policy changes materially (for example, a new network feature is added), we will notify you prominently within the App (via dialog or banner), and the update takes effect after your confirmation.
