
# [UnicornPlayer App] Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

## I. Preface

We understand the importance of personal information to you and will do our utmost to keep it safe and reliable. This Privacy Policy applies throughout the process of downloading, installing, registering, logging in, and using this App. We handle your personal information in strict compliance with relevant laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China, including but not limited to the Personal Information Protection Law, the Cybersecurity Law, and the Data Security Law.

As an independent individual developer, we promise:
- We will never collect personal information unrelated to the core functionality beyond the necessary scope;
- We will never share your personal information with any third party without your consent;
- All data processing practices follow the principles of legality, propriety, necessity, and good faith.

## II. Definitions

1. **Personal Information:** Various information recorded electronically or otherwise that can identify a specific natural person, either alone or in combination with other information. Does not include anonymized information.
2. **Sensitive Personal Information:** Personal information that, if leaked or used illegally, may easily infringe upon a natural person's dignity or endanger their personal and property safety, including biometric data, financial accounts, location tracking information, etc.
3. **Anonymization:** The process of processing personal information so that a specific natural person can no longer be identified, and the process is irreversible.

## III. Information We Collect and Purpose

We only collect the minimum amount of information necessary to implement this App's core features, and we will never collect redundant data unrelated to functionality:

1. **Basic Device Information:** We only collect device model, operating system version, and anonymized device identifiers — for troubleshooting app crashes and optimizing compatibility across different device models.
2. **Information Corresponding to Feature-Required Permissions:**
   - If you use the export feature, we only access your local photo album after you explicitly grant permission — to save the files you generate. We do not upload any other unrelated content from your album.
   - If you use the voice input feature, we only invoke the microphone after you explicitly grant permission. Voice content is recognized and processed entirely on-device and is never uploaded to any server.
3. **Information You Voluntarily Submit:** If you send us problem descriptions through the feedback channel, we only collect contact details you voluntarily provide — for replying to you. This information is used solely for customer communication purposes.

We promise that all information we collect is used only for the purposes explicitly stated above and will not be used for any other undisclosed purposes.

## IV. Data Storage and Protection

1. **Storage Location:** All non-anonymized personal information is stored on compliant cloud servers within the territory of the People's Republic of China. No personal user data is transmitted outside the country.
2. **Retention Period:** We retain your information only for the period necessary to fulfill the functional purpose. After you deactivate your account or actively delete data, we will completely remove all related data within 15 working days.
3. **Security Measures:** We protect your personal information using TLS encrypted transmission and AES encrypted storage. At the same time, we strictly restrict data access permissions — only the developer has access to backend data.
4. **Security Incident Response:** In the event of a personal information security incident, we will promptly notify you via an in-App popup of the incident details and remedial actions, and report to the competent regulatory authority as required by law.

## V. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We promise that, except in the following two circumstances, we will never share, transfer, or publicly disclose your personal information to any third party:
1. After you give your separate and explicit consent, we will share the specific information you authorize with the designated third party.
2. In accordance with lawful and mandatory requirements from judicial or administrative authorities, we will disclose relevant information to the minimum extent necessary, and notify you in advance to the extent permitted by law.

For third-party services integrated into this App (such as cloud storage, analytics tools), we have signed strict data protection agreements with them, requiring that they process your information only for the purpose of providing their services and not repurpose it.

## VI. Your Personal Information Rights

1. **Right of Access and Correction:** You can access and correct your personal information at any time in the App's settings page.
2. **Right of Deletion:** You can apply to delete all your personal data at any time in the settings page. We will complete the processing within 15 working days.
3. **Right to Deactivate Account:** You can submit an account deactivation application through the deactivation entry in the App's settings page. We will complete the deactivation and remove all related data within 15 working days.
4. **Right to Revoke Authorization:** You can disable corresponding permission authorizations at any time in your phone's system settings. After disabling, we will no longer collect the information corresponding to that permission, and disabling will not affect your use of other basic App features.

## VII. Minor Protection

This App is intended for users of all ages. We do not actively collect any personal information from minors under the age of 14. If you are a minor, please use this application only with the supervision of your guardian.

## VIII. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as required by changes in laws and regulations or adjustments to our features. After an update, we will notify you via a prominent popup within the App, and the revised policy takes effect only after you confirm and agree.
